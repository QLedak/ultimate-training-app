# Accelerate: 12-Week Off-Season Speed Program (design)

4 days per week, comfortable lifting level, no conditioning, no weights prescribed (athletes enter the first set's weight; effort ratings adjust later sets).

**Sprint reps per week (starts, resisted and flying sprints):** W1: 12, W2: 14, W3: 16, W4: 8, W5: 14, W6: 17, W7: 18, W8: 8, W9: 10, W10: 12, W11: 10, W12: 6
